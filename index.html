Nice to meet you! How did you get into design?

It started with a simple belief: great products come from truly understanding the people who use them.

I began in UX research — talking to users, uncovering pain points, and understanding the behaviours and influences behind their decisions. That still shapes how I design today.

Then I got curious about what happens after research. I wanted to solve the problems I was uncovering — so I moved into product design: flows, wireframes, prototypes and continuous validation.

How do you approach a new problem?

To me, end-to-end design means staying close to the problem from start to finish. It's a bit like a good rally — read the play first, then make the shot.

  1. 1Understand peopleUser research, interviews, competitor analysis
  2. 2Find the frictionHeuristic evaluation, behaviour & drop-off analysis
  3. 3Shape the solutionInformation architecture, flows, wireframes, prototypes, design systems
  4. 4Validate & shipUsability testing, experiments, iteration
