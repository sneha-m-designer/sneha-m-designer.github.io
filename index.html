About me

Great products come from truly understanding the people who use them.

After a B.E. in Computer Science and a stint in cybersecurity at HCL, I began my design career in UX research. Wanting to solve the problems I uncovered led me into product design.

Today I design end to end for AI-powered B2B SaaS and B2C products, and I measure what changes.
