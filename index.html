My journey into design started with a simple belief: great products come from truly understanding the people who use them. I began in UX research, and that foundation still shapes how I design today.

I've built products from the ground up and improved existing ones. I love finding friction points, simplifying workflows and making experiences more intuitive — and it's most rewarding when those improvements create measurable impact.

“End-to-end product design means staying close to the problem from start to finish — understanding users deeply, shaping solutions thoughtfully, and seeing ideas through until they become real products.”
