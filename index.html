My story

Great products come from truly understanding the people who use them.

  1. 🎓
    The foundation · 2017 – 2023

    Engineering, then the real world

    I completed my B.E. in Computer Science Engineering at Kamaraj College of Engineering and Technology with an 8.25 CGPA, then started my career in cybersecurity at HCL — where I learned stakeholder management, Agile delivery and systems thinking.

    B.E., Computer Science EngineeringKamaraj College of Engineering and Technology · 2017 – 2021 · CGPA 8.25
  2. 🔍
    Research first · 2023

    A simple belief

    My journey into design started with a simple belief: great products come from truly understanding the people who use them. That's why I began my design career in UX research — talking to users, uncovering pain points, understanding behaviours, and learning what influences their decisions. Those experiences still shape the way I design today.

  3. ✏️
    Into product design · 2024

    From insights to experiences

    As I gained experience, I became curious about what happens after the research. I wanted to help solve the problems I was uncovering. That led me into product design, where I turn insights into experiences through user flows, wireframes, prototypes and continuous validation.

  4. 🚀
    Building & improving · today

    Finding friction, measuring impact

    I've worked on products from the ground up and improved existing ones. I enjoy identifying friction points, simplifying workflows, and creating experiences that feel easier and more intuitive. The most rewarding part is seeing those improvements create a measurable impact.

  5. 🎯
    What drives me

    End to end, close to the problem

    To me, end-to-end product design means staying close to the problem from start to finish: understanding users deeply, shaping solutions thoughtfully, and seeing ideas through until they become real products.

If you enjoy building products that solve meaningful problems and put users at the centre, I'd love to connect.
