my story

Great products start with understanding people

My journey into design started with a simple belief: great products come from truly understanding the people who use them.

I began in UX research — talking to users, uncovering pain points, and understanding the behaviours and influences behind their decisions. That foundation still shapes how I design today.

Over time I grew curious about what happens after research. I wanted to solve the problems I was uncovering, which led me into product design: user flows, wireframes, prototypes and continuous validation.

I've built products from the ground up and improved existing ones. I love finding friction points, simplifying workflows and making experiences more intuitive — and it's most rewarding when those improvements create measurable impact.

To me, end-to-end product design means staying close to the problem from start to finish: understanding users deeply, shaping solutions thoughtfully, and seeing ideas through until they become real products.
