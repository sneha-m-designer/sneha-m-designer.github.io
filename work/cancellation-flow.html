01 · Overview

A sensitive moment, made harder than it needed to be

At Two Minute Reports, users couldn't cancel their subscription on their own. They had to contact support, wait for a reply, fill out a cancellation form, and then wait again for confirmation. This added friction to an already sensitive moment in the user journey.

The business also had a blind spot: cancellation feedback was collected through a generic form, so it was hard to understand why users were really leaving. I redesigned the experience with an in-app, self-serve cancellation flow and a structured feedback system that turned churn into actionable insight.

02 · The problem

Four steps, two waits, zero control

  1. 1Contact support
  2. 2Wait for a response⏳
  3. 3Fill out a cancellation form
  4. 4Wait for confirmation⏳
The result

Frustrated users, heavy support dependency, and delayed task completion.

The hidden cost

Generic feedback that couldn't explain why users were churning.

03 · Research & findings

I analysed feedback from 500+ cancelled users

Cancellation reasons fell into 8 recurring categories:

  • 🧩Product limitations
  • 💸Pricing concerns
  • 🔌Missing integrations
  • 🔀Switching to alternative tools
  • ✅Temporary business need completed
  • 😤Cancellation experience issues
  • 📦Wrong plan selection
  • 🎧Customer support concerns

Key insights — the biggest drivers of churn

  • Users expected more advanced workflows
  • Missing connectors such as Stripe, Salesforce, Threads and Snapchat
  • A preference for competing tools
  • Campaigns or client projects coming to an end
  • Enterprise requirements that didn't match product capabilities

04 · The opportunity

From a flow that processes cancellations to one that learns from them

  • ⚡Reduce frictionthrough self-serve cancellation
  • 🔍Capture better churn insightsat the moment of exit
  • 💜Spot retention opportunitiesbefore users leave
  • 🎧Reduce support workloadno tickets needed to cancel

05 · The solution

Three changes that turned an exit into insight

1

In-app cancellation

Users can now cancel directly inside the product, without contacting support. This removes the dependency on the support team and gives users full control over their subscription.

2

Personalised feedback collection

Instead of asking everyone the same question, the follow-up questions now adapt to the reason the user selects — turning generic responses into actionable business intelligence.

  • Pricing concerns→Pricing-related questions
  • Missing features→Product-specific questions
  • Switching to another tool→Competitor insights
3

A better offboarding experience

The new flow gives users a clear, transparent path to cancel, while helping the business understand their intent.

BeforeSupport-led4 steps · 2 waits · generic form
AfterSelf-serve, in-appNo support needed · questions tailored to each reason
