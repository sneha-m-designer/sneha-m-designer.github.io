Rebuilding the final step of the free flow so users complete their task faster and see the value of upgrading.

01 · Overview

The most engaged users, and the upgrade barely registered

Free users who reach the Download Page have already completed the entire editing flow. They're the most engaged users in the product, and the best audience for an upgrade. Yet only 2.36% of them clicked “Try Automatic Plan.”

The page was cluttered, the main action was hard to find, low-value actions looked the most important, and the upgrade sat as a small text item. I redesigned it to put the download first, show users their final photo, and introduce the paid plan through a visual nudge that sparks curiosity instead of getting ignored.

02 · The problem

The page fought the user's goal — and the business's

  • The primary action wasn't prominent: Download was a small icon in a list, though it's why users are there
  • Low-value actions looked primary: “Buy me a coffee” (0.56% usage) and “Spread the word” (0.05%) dominated the page
  • The upgrade didn't look like a CTA: “Automatic – Try for FREE” read as an icon with a description
  • No preview before download: users only saw their edited photo after downloading the file
The result

A high-intent moment was wasted: users downloaded and left, or got distracted by actions that didn't matter.

The real goal

Help users finish their task — and give them a reason to pause and consider the paid plan.

Root causes

  1. Low visibilityThe upgrade competed with many elements and wasn't prominent
  2. Weak value communicationNo strong reason was given to consider upgrading
  3. No clear differentiationUsers couldn't see how paid output would beat what they had
  4. Task focusUsers came to download; nothing gave them a reason to pause
  5. Poor timingThe upgrade was passive, not shown where its value was clear

04 · The opportunity

The end of the journey is when users are most invested

  • ⬇️Make the primary task effortlessgive Download the prominence it deserves
  • 👀Show value, don't describe ita visual preview of the paid result
  • 📐Fix the action hierarchyso the page reflects what users actually do
  • ✨Make upgrading an active momentwithout blocking the download

06 · The solution

Download first, then a reason to look twice

Each number on the new page maps to a decision below.

  1. 1

    Download is the clear primary action

    “Download your photo” is now a large, high-contrast button — the most prominent thing on the page — with a “Your photo is ready” confirmation that they've reached the finish line.

  2. 2

    A preview before download

    Users see their final photo laid out on the selected paper size (e.g. 6 x 4 inch / 4R) right on the page, instead of finding out only after downloading.

  3. 3

    A fixed action hierarchy

    “Try Again”, the second most-used action, became “Create another image” just below Download. “Spread the word” and “Buy me a coffee” moved to a quiet bottom row, and Professional Prints was removed until it's available.

  4. 4

    A visual upgrade nudge

    A blurred professional photo labelled “Unlock with Automatic” lets users imagine the better result instead of reading about it — backed by quick chips: perfect lighting, face centered, clean background, biometric compliant.

  5. 5

    A clear, low-risk offer

    ₹99 with ₹132 struck through and 25% OFF (+18% GST), consistent with the plan selection redesign. 2 biometric photos and 7 photo grids, zero wait, instant download, no watermarks — a green “Try for Free” button and 5.5 million+ photos created as proof.

08 · Key learning

Show value, don't just describe it.

The users most likely to upgrade are the ones who've already done the work. Instead of interrupting them, the best upsell respects their goal — then shows them, at a glance, what a better result could look like.
