How I redesigned plan selection to grow AI plan selection from under 1% to 6%

Moving users from low-intent, early decisions to a clear, value-driven choice between Free and Paid.

01 · Overview

Most users defaulted to Free — before they knew what Paid offered

At MakePassportPhoto, fewer than 1% of users converted to a paid plan. Users were asked to choose between Free and Paid at the very start of the flow, before they understood what either option offered. With no clear difference between plans, and key information hidden behind small info icons, most users simply defaulted to Free.

I redesigned the plan selection experience to make the paid plan's value obvious, make the price feel like a deal, and help users make a confident, informed choice.

02 · The problem

Three look-alike options, chosen too early

Users had to pick a plan at the entry point, before seeing the value of AI-powered output. This created friction because users:

  • Didn't clearly understand the benefits of the paid plan
  • Defaulted to Free because the plans looked too similar
  • Never saw the value of the AI output before deciding
  • Had to open a separate info icon for each plan to find out what it included
The result

Low-intent decisions made early in the journey, keeping paid conversion below 1%.

The hidden cost

Once a user chose Free, there was no way to switch to Paid. They had to start over.

03 · Research & findings

Funnel data told me what — session recordings told me why

📊 Quantitative · PostHog

  • Paid conversion rate was below 1%
  • The majority of users selected the Free plan at entry
  • Fewer than 1% of users even selected the AI plan

🎥 Qualitative · Session recordings

  • Users picked a plan quickly without exploring the details
  • Very few users clicked any of the “info (i)” icons
  • No visible comparison between Free and Paid at the moment of decision
  • Users who chose Free had no path to upgrade without restarting

04 · The opportunity

The screen asked users to commit before showing them why

  • 👀Make the paid plan's value visibleat the moment of decision
  • 🏷️Reframe pricingso it feels like a deal, not a cost
  • 🧠Reduce cognitive loadby making plans easy to compare
  • 🎯Guide attention to conversioninstead of open-ended exploration

05 · The solution

Six decisions, one clearer screen

Each number on the new screen maps to a decision below.

→
  1. 1

    Highlighted the paid plan

    The Automatic (AI) plan is now pre-selected, framed in a bordered card with a “Recommended” badge, and placed above the free option. Free sits below in a quieter card, so the hierarchy is clear at a glance.

  2. 2

    Benefits in one line

    Each plan carries a single line explaining what users get. “Pay only if satisfied” also removes the risk of paying upfront.

  3. 3

    Discounted pricing

    ₹99 with ₹132 struck through and a 25% OFF badge. Charm pricing makes the price feel lighter, and GST is shown separately (+18% GST) so the headline price stays clean. Separate pricing was designed for non-Indian users.

  4. 4

    Clarity in every selection

    Labelled “Country” and “Document” fields side by side, with placeholders instead of pre-selected values and helper text confirming the choice. Renamed the paid plan “Automatic (AI)” to explain what makes it different, and removed the Print option until it's available.

  5. 5

    Easy plan comparison

    One info icon beside “Order Option” opens a single modal comparing both plans side by side — instead of one modal per plan.

  6. 6

    Rewrote a misleading CTA

    “GET PHOTO” made users think they were at the final step. “Let's Get Started” sets the right expectation for what comes next.

06 · Impact

From a default click to a confident choice

📈 What changed

  • 6×+ increase in users choosing the paid AI plan at entry
  • Paid conversion crossed 1% for the first time
  • Users understand the difference between plans at the moment of decision — without opening multiple modals

🔭 What's next

  • Upgrade nudges in the Free editing flow: the Free plan has four editing steps — a well-placed nudge to “generate your photo in one click” could convert users mid-journey
  • A reusable comparison modal: one Free vs Paid component that can appear anywhere in the product

07 · Key learning

Users can't value what they can't see.

Asking people to choose a plan before they understand it leads to default choices, not real decisions. Making value visible, framing price as a deal, and reducing effort at the moment of choice turned a low-intent click into a confident decision — and grew AI plan selection more than sixfold.
